        Settings

        Equalizer

        Shape the sound with 10 bands. Double-click a slider to reset it.

        Normalize volume

        Plays every song at the same loudness, so you don't have to keep changing the volume.

        Skip to vocals

        Starts each song 5 seconds before the singing begins and moves on 5 seconds after it ends. Works once a song's lyrics have been processed.

        Crossfade

        Blends the end of each song into the start of the next one when the queue moves on by itself. Set it to 0 to turn it off.

        De-esser

        Softens harsh "s" and "t" sounds. Auto finds the harsh frequency in each song for you.

        Show queue

        Keep the queue panel open next to your music.

        Playlist cover collage

        Show the covers of a playlist's first 4 songs as its picture.

        Relay To Microphone

        Mix the music Wave plays into your microphone, so people in Discord and other apps hear it.

        Run on startup

        Open Wave automatically when you sign in to Windows.

        Discord

        Show what you're listening to on your Discord profile.
